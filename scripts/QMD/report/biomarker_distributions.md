# Introduction

This report presents the distribution of patient characteristics
stratified by treatment arm and biomarker status. Three biomarker
strategies are evaluated:

- **CRP**: C-reactive protein (positive: CRP \< 5)
- **TLR**: Tumor lesion reduction (positive: TLR $\geq$ 10%)
- **TMB/BRAF**: Combined biomarker (positive: TMB $\geq$ 9 mut/MB OR
  BRAF mutation)

Each table shows the distribution of age, sex, and the other two
biomarkers (both categorical and continuous) within subgroups defined by
treatment arm and biomarker status.
